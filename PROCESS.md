# Tools Used and Their Purposes
I used Codex during the planning and writing process of this article. At the beginning, I had it explain the assignment requirements. After that, I used Codex to test my initial arguments and help me build the framework and outline of the article. While writing, I first expressed my thoughts in Chinese, and then asked Codex to help me translate them into English and correct the grammar. Then, based on what I really wanted to express, I continued to adjust the structure, language, and arguments of the article.

I refused to use this point as the core argument of the article because it was too broad and did not fully express my true stance.
